Why Volunteer With Us?

Serve with Purpose

Your efforts directly care for and protect deserving cows.

Inner Growth

Seva purifies the heart and brings true happiness.

Community & Connection

Join a family of like-minded people working for a higher cause.

Learn & Grow

Gain new skills, learn traditions and contribute to rural upliftment.

Make a Lasting Impact

Together, we build a kinder, more compassionate world.

Ways You Can Volunteer

Cow Care & Feeding

Help in daily feeding, grooming and caring for our cows.

Hospital Support

Assist in cleaning, maintenance and keeping the shelter hygienic.

Medical & Veterinary

Support veterinary camps and help in animal healthcare.

Community Outreach

Educate and connect with rural communities and children.

Events & Campaigns

Be a part of awareness drives, events and special campaigns.

Admin & Creative

Help with documentation, design, social media and administrative tasks.
